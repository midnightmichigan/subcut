# SubCut — demo video script (< 3 min)

Target: ~2 minutes. Screen-record the browser at 1080p. No facecam needed; cursor + narration.
Narration in *italics*, shot/action in plain text.

---

**[0:00–0:15] Hook — the problem**
*Open on the SubCut input screen.*
- *Show the headline on screen. Read it:* "Find every subscription draining your bank account."
- *Say:* "The average person underestimates what they spend on subscriptions by two to three times. Free trials convert, prices creep up, and the money just… leaks. Existing tools want your bank login to help. SubCut doesn't — your statement never leaves this tab."

**[0:15–0:35] Input — paste or demo**
- *Click "Load demo statement". Scroll the textarea briefly to show realistic statement lines.*
- *Say:* "I'll use the built-in demo — four months of realistic statement text, the way you'd copy it from any bank. Or paste your own. Deposits and paychecks are ignored automatically."

**[0:35–1:05] The reveal — leak report**
- *Click "Find my leaks". Let the dashboard render. Pause on the hero stats.*
- *Say:* "Ten subscriptions found. $143.96 leaking every month — that's $1,727.56 a year. Each card shows the merchant, the amount, how often it bills, and when it first appeared."
- *Scroll the cards. Hover the "Detected pattern" badge on Streambox Premium.*
- *Say:* "Known merchants are verified — and this one, Streambox Premium, was caught by the pattern detector: same charge, every month, a merchant name nobody would recognize. That's the forgotten free trial."

**[1:05–1:25] The urgent flag**
- *Scroll to the Amazon Prime card with the red "Renews in 12 days" flag.*
- *Say:* "And this is the one that pays for the whole app: a $139 annual charge renewing in twelve days that most people wouldn't notice until the money's gone."

**[1:25–1:50] Action — cancel flow**
- *Toggle three subscriptions to Cancel. Watch the savings number climb.*
- *Say:* "Mark what you don't want… and there's your number: real yearly savings, live."
- *Expand the Prime card. Show the cancel steps. Click "Copy cancel script". Show the toast.*
- *Say:* "Every subscription gets step-by-step cancel instructions and a copyable cancellation script. No automation, no funny business — just everything you need to pull the plug."

**[1:50–2:05] Privacy close**
- *Open DevTools → Network tab briefly (or just gesture to the privacy badge).*
- *Say:* "And the part I'm proudest of: zero network requests. Your financial text physically cannot leave your browser — check the Network tab yourself. SubCut: found it, cancel it, keep the money."

**[End card]** SubCut · GitHub repo link · "Built with the Devpost Learn Skill Pack"

---

**Recording notes:** Record in one continuous take if possible; narration can be added in post. Keep cursor movements slow and deliberate. Zoom browser to 125% for readability. No copyrighted music — silent or original audio only.
